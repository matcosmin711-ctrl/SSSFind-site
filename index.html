Search wider. Compare better. Find the lowest verified price.

SSSFind searches relevant public online sources, verifies original offer pages and compares prices so users can quickly reach the lowest verified offer found among the sources SSSFind can search and verify.

How SSSFind works

Search across sources

SSSFind discovers offers across online retailers, marketplaces and public web sources instead of relying on a single shop.

Verify original offers

Discovery results are checked against the original product or listing page before an offer is presented as verified.

Keep searching lower

Paid searches can continue in the background during a bounded search round while SSSFind compares verified offers for a lower price.
